# Preserve owner controls across briefing differences

Date: 8 October 2026. Status: Open conflicts recorded; no authority expanded.

## Decision and consequences

The authorised branch policy permits only main and development. Additional feature branches require explicit approval. Preserve the two-branch policy while establishing repository access. Do not automatically require an impossible owner self-review on an owner-authored PR. Hosted protection design needs actual account capabilities. Also retain strict reapproval after edits and do not implement auto-publication until explicitly resolved. UAT/production topology requires review before cloud work. These conflicts do not block read-only contract implementation.
