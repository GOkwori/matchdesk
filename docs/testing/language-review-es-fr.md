# Spanish and French review sheet

Every Spanish and French string a visitor can see in the stories, recaps, overlays and replay, with its English source, for a native speaker to check. They are AI-assisted templates in `matchdesk.domain.story_feed`, `recap_feed`, `overlay_feed` and the studio's `story-chrome.ts` and `recap-chrome.ts`. Numbers, names and times are filled in from verified claims; only the wording around them needs review.

For each row record **OK**, **Fix** (with the corrected text) or **Wrong meaning**, and sign at the end. A spreadsheet with the same rows and a verdict column is shared with reviewers. The studio shows "AI-assisted translation, native-speaker review pending" until a signed sheet is recorded in the evidence index.
