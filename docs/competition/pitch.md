# Project pitch

I am building MatchDesk, an explainable AI football production desk. My aim is to turn
synthetic match events into stories a producer can inspect before publishing, with
each factual claim linked to its supporting events and calculations.

The repository now includes a deterministic synthetic match engine, registered football
metrics, evidence-linked claim verification, bounded specialist orchestration contracts,
versioned producer review and audience-variant controls, plus a Next.js studio and
isolated resettable local judge demo. The judge demonstration uses scripted specialist
copy and unreviewed multilingual examples, not live model-produced narrative.

Real delegated workforce sign-in, secure producer BFF, live multilingual evaluation,
independent language and accessibility review, production deployment and broadcast
publication remain separately gated. CI qualification is not permission to publish.
