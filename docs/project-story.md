# Why I am building MatchDesk

I want a football story to come with an explanation of its evidence. A scoreline
can describe the result, but it does not show a producer how a particular passage
of play supports the words they are about to publish.

My initiative brings those parts together: synthetic match events, deterministic
statistics, specialist interpretation and a producer-controlled publishing desk.
I want selecting a sentence to reveal the events and calculations behind it.

I am keeping the early build focused on the boundaries that make that possible.
The current foundation validates an event's structure and protects its identity.
It does not pretend that a valid JSON event is a verified football claim.

The intended next step is a reproducible match engine. Once the data and calculations
are testable, the narrative workflow can operate against a defined source of truth.
I am not claiming broadcast customer validation, a deployed product or production
performance at this stage. The evidence record will track what the build actually proves.
